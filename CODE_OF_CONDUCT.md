# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a calm, technically rigorous, and welcoming place to work. Contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and observable behavior;
- explain disagreement with evidence and leave room to revise conclusions;
- protect credentials, private terminal output, and other sensitive information;
- make the project accessible to people with different levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Please report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub security contact or to the maintainer. Include the relevant link or context, what happened, and what outcome you need. Reports will be reviewed in confidence as far as practical.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep collaboration safe and productive.
